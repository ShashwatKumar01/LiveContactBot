## 🌟 Why LiveContact Bot?

Are you a **channel admin**, **crypto community owner**, **influencer**, or **business** looking for a safe, professional way for followers to message you? 

Sharing your personal `@username` exposes you to spam, scams, and direct harassment. **LiveContact Bot** (`@ReplyDmBot`) allows you to connect your own custom Telegram bot in seconds. When someone messages your bot, it forwards their message straight to your private chat. To reply, you simply tap **"Reply"** on the forwarded message — fast, seamless, and 100% private!

> ⚡ **Modern Livegram Alternative**: Livegram Bot frequently experiences downtime, lag, and strict limits. LiveContact Bot offers high-speed async processing, multi-bot management, instant replies, and rich media support.

## 🛠️ Quick Start Guide (Ready in 30 Seconds)

### Step 1: Create a Bot with @BotFather
1. Open [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather) on Telegram.
2. Send `/newbot` and follow the prompts to choose a name and username (e.g. `MyChannelFeedback_bot`).
3. Copy the **HTTP API Bot Token** provided by BotFather.

### Step 2: Connect to LiveContact Bot
1. Open [@ReplyDmBot](https://t.me/ReplyDmBot) on Telegram and send `/start`.
2. Tap **➕ Add Bot** (or send `/addbot`).
3. Paste your Bot Token or forward the BotFather message.
4. Your custom contact bot is now live and running!

### Step 3: Start Chatting!
Share your new bot's link (`t.me/YourBotName`) with your channel audience. When users message your bot, you receive their messages instantly in your private chat. **Simply reply to any message to send an answer!**
